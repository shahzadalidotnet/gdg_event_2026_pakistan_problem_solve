DGI&P

Renew / apply for a passport

Verified Last verified 14 Aug 2026 Source: dgip.gov.pk
Government fee
36-page 5-yr MRP: Rs. 4500 (Normal) / Rs. 7500 (Urgent) · 36-page 10-yr: Rs. 6700 / Rs. 11200
Processing time
Normal 10–15 working days · Urgent 4–7 working days
Office hours (verified)
RPOs: Mon–Fri 8AM–1:30PM for new tokens (biometrics run later)
How collection works
Not same-day — track online and collect from the office or receive by courier per your chosen speed.

Reality check — what actually happens

The stuff the official site won't tell you, from people who've been there.

  • Reach before 8 AM at busy offices — the daily token quota can finish in the first two hours.
  • The 10-year passport costs more upfront but saves a future renewal — worth it if you travel often.
  • You can pay online by card or at an e-Sahulat franchise — no need to queue at a bank.
  • Your photo must match DGIP spec (no glasses, plain background) or it's re-taken there.
  • Carry the original CNIC of your father/spouse if names need cross-verification.

Documents you need

  • Original CNIC + photocopy
  • Previous passport (for renewal)
  • Online appointment confirmation / fee challan
  • 2 passport-size photos as per DGIP spec

Step by step

  1. Apply online or book an appointment
    Complete your application on the DGIP online portal (onlinemrp) or book a slot at a passport office.
  2. Pay the passport fee
    Pay online by Visa/Master card, or physically at a designated bank or e-Sahulat franchise. Keep the challan.
  3. Visit for biometrics & photo
    Attend your slot for fingerprints, photo and data verification.
  4. Complete verification
    The verification counter confirms your particulars.
  5. Collect or receive by courier
    Track via the portal; collect from the office or receive by courier per your chosen speed.
