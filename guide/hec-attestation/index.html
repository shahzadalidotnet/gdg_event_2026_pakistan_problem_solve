HEC

Attest your degree (HEC e-attestation)

Verified Last verified 14 Aug 2026 Source: eservices.hec.gov.pk
Government fee
Rs. 3000 per document (degree, transcript, provisional certificate or equivalence letter)
Processing time
10–15 working days total — ~5–8 days university verification + 3–5 days HEC review
Office hours (verified)
e-portal 24/7 · Regional Centres: Mon–Fri 9AM–4PM
How collection works
Now largely digital — the e-attestation certificate is downloaded from your HEC dashboard. A centre visit (biometrics/originals) is only required if your case needs it.

Reality check — what actually happens

The stuff the official site won't tell you, from people who've been there.

  • The fee is now Rs. 3000 per document — guides quoting Rs. 800 are out of date.
  • Remove plastic lamination before scanning/attesting — stamps won't stick to plastic.
  • List every qualification from Matric onwards or the application stalls at verification.
  • University verification is the slow, out-of-your-control part — apply well before any deadline.
  • Bring at least 2 photocopies of every document if you're called to a Regional Centre.

Documents you need

  • Scanned copy (PDF/JPEG) of each degree, transcript & certificate — Matric onwards
  • Original documents (bring to your Regional Centre appointment)
  • CNIC / passport copy
  • Printed e-portal appointment confirmation
  • Remove any plastic lamination before scanning/attesting

Step by step

  1. Create an account on the HEC e-portal
    Sign up at eservices.hec.gov.pk (open 24/7) and complete your academic profile — list every qualification from Matric onwards.
  2. Upload your documents
    Attach scanned PDFs/JPEGs of each degree, transcript and certificate you want attested.
  3. Pay the fee (Rs. 3000 per document)
    Pay via 1-Link — mobile banking, ATM, or any bank branch using the generated consumer number.
  4. University verification
    HEC sends your records to the awarding university to confirm — usually 5–8 working days and out of your hands.
  5. Book your appointment
    Once verified, you get an SMS/email to pick a date and an HEC Regional Centre (Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, etc.).
  6. Get your e-attestation
    After final review an e-attestation certificate is issued — download it from your dashboard; SMS/email confirms.
