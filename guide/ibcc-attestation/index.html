IBCC

Attest / get equivalence (IBCC)

Verified Last verified 14 Aug 2026 Source: ibcc.edu.pk
Government fee
Attestation: ~Rs. 1200 per original certificate · ~Rs. 800 per photocopy · Board verification ~Rs. 600 + Rs. 300 courier. Equivalence is a separate, higher fee.
Processing time
Attestation: same day (in person) · Board verification & equivalence: several working days
Office hours (verified)
Regional offices: Mon–Fri 9AM–1PM for submissions (verify on-site — not Google's listing)
How collection works
Submit in the morning, then come back the SAME DAY around 3–5 PM to collect attested docs. Equivalence takes several days — collect on the printed date or by courier.

Reality check — what actually happens

The stuff the official site won't tell you, from people who've been there.

  • Attestation ≠ equivalence: attestation stamps your Pakistani certificate; equivalence converts a FOREIGN qualification to its Pakistani equivalent. They are separate applications with different fees.
  • Board verification comes first — IBCC won't attest a matric/inter certificate the board hasn't verified.
  • Google's timing is frequently wrong — counters stop accepting new files ~1 hour before closing.
  • You submit in the morning and collect the same evening — plan for two trips in one day, not one.
  • Bring photocopies of BOTH the student's and father's CNIC even when the online list is vague.
  • Fees are per document and add up fast — ~Rs. 1200 per original + ~Rs. 800 per photocopy.

Documents you need

  • Printed application form from the IBCC portal
  • Original certificate + one photocopy of each
  • Additional photocopies of every document to be attested
  • Photocopies of the student's and father's CNIC / B-Form
  • Paid fee challan or online payment receipt
  • For equivalence: original foreign qualification + transcripts

Step by step

  1. Get board verification first
    Before IBCC can attest, your matric/intermediate certificate must be verified by the issuing education board (~Rs. 600 + courier).
  2. Apply & book on the IBCC portal
    Fill the application at ibcc.edu.pk, book an appointment, and print the form.
  3. Pay the fee
    Pay the per-document attestation (or equivalence) fee via bank/approved channel and keep the challan.
  4. Submit at the IBCC office (or by courier)
    Hand over originals + photocopies + form + challan; the counter verifies and issues a receipt.
  5. Processing
    Attestation is same-day; equivalence goes for evaluation and takes several working days.
  6. Collect or receive by courier
    For attestation, collect the same day (usually evening); for equivalence, collect on the printed date or by courier.
