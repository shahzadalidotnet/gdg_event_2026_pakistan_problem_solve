Islamabad Traffic Police (ITP)

Get a driving licence (Islamabad)

Verified Last verified 14 Aug 2026 Source: islamabadpolice.gov.pk/itp
Government fee
Learner permit Rs. 1000 (6-month) · Medical Rs. 200 · Road test Rs. 200 · Smart card Rs. 2000
Processing time
Learner: same day · Permanent: apply after the 42-day learner period
Office hours (verified)
Licensing offices: Mon–Fri 9AM–3PM (test slots fill by noon)
How collection works
Learner permit is issued same-day. The permanent smart card is couriered or collected after processing — not same-day.

Reality check — what actually happens

The stuff the official site won't tell you, from people who've been there.

  • You no longer need to visit ITP just to pay — generate a PSID on DLIMS and pay via EasyPaisa/JazzCash/bank.
  • Learner permit is now Rs. 1000 (6-month) — older guides quoting ~Rs. 60 are outdated.
  • Google timings are often wrong — the test track stops taking new candidates around 12:30 PM.
  • Carry the original medical certificate; a photocopy is not accepted.
  • If your CNIC address is outside Islamabad, bring a local utility bill or tenancy agreement.

Documents you need

  • Original CNIC + photocopy
  • Original learner permit (for the permanent licence)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Medical fitness certificate (Rs. 200)
  • Local utility bill / tenancy agreement if your CNIC address is outside Islamabad

Step by step

  1. Generate a PSID & pay online
    Create a PSID on the DLIMS portal and pay via EasyPaisa, JazzCash, internet banking, ATM or any E-Khidmat Center — no need to visit ITP just to pay.
  2. Apply for the learner permit
    Submit your application with CNIC. The learner permit is valid 6 months; display an 'L' sign while practising.
  3. Wait the mandatory 42 days
    You must hold the learner permit for at least 42 days before you can take the driving test.
  4. Book your test slot
    Book online via DLIMS or in person; bring the learner permit and your documents.
  5. Pass theory + practical test
    A signs/signals test, then the computerized on-track road test (Rs. 200) with an examiner.
  6. Biometrics, pay & collect
    Give biometrics, pay the smart card fee (Rs. 2000); the card is printed and couriered or collected.
