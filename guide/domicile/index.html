Reality check — what actually happens

The stuff the official site won't tell you, from people who've been there.

  • Requirements vary by district — confirm the exact list at your DC office or provincial e-portal first.
  • Punjab, Sindh and Islamabad now have online domicile e-services — check before queuing.
  • The affidavit must be on the correct stamp paper, attested by an oath commissioner.
  • A police/patwari residence check is part of the process — be reachable at your stated address.
  • Bring the original CNIC of your father/husband, not just yours — it's checked for lineage.

Documents you need

  • CNIC (or B-Form if under 18) — original + copy
  • Father's / husband's CNIC copy
  • Two passport-size photos
  • Proof of residence — utility bill or property papers
  • Educational certificate (e.g. Matric)
  • Affidavit on stamp paper (attested by an oath commissioner)

Step by step

  1. Get the form (office or online)
    Collect the domicile form from the DC office, or apply via your province's e-services portal — Punjab, Sindh and ICT have online systems.
  2. Prepare documents & affidavit
    Attach CNIC/B-Form, residence proof, photos, educational certificate and the affidavit on stamp paper.
  3. Submit at the counter & pay
    Submit the file at the designated counter and pay the fee (Rs. 200–500); collect your receipt/token.
  4. Residence verification
    Local police or the area patwari verify that you actually reside in the district.
  5. Collect the certificate
    Once verified, the DC office issues the signed domicile — collect on the date on your receipt.
