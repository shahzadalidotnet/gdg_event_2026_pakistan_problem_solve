NADRA

Renew your CNIC (Smart Card)

Verified Last verified 14 Aug 2026 Source: id.nadra.gov.pk
Government fee
Smart CNIC: Rs. 1000 (Normal) · Rs. 2000 (Urgent) · Rs. 3000 (Executive) · + Rs. 165 courier
Processing time
Normal 30 · Urgent 20 · Executive 10 working days
Office hours (verified)
Mega Centers: Mon–Sat 8AM–8PM · NRCs: Mon–Fri 9AM–5PM · Pak-ID app 24/7
How collection works
Not same-day. The card is couriered to your address or collected from the NRC on the date printed on your token — only the paper token is issued same-day.

Reality check — what actually happens

The stuff the official site won't tell you, from people who've been there.

  • If nothing has changed, renew fully online via the Pak-ID app — no queue and the card is delivered home.
  • Token counters usually stop issuing new tokens ~1 hour before closing — reach early.
  • Bring the original CNIC even for a renewal; a photocopy alone is refused at the counter.
  • Smart Card fees start at Rs. 1000 — older guides quoting Rs. 750 are the outdated non-smart rate.
  • The Executive counter is a separate, faster queue if you pay the top fee.

Documents you need

  • Your existing CNIC (original) — bring it even if expired
  • One recent passport-size photo with a white background
  • Supporting document ONLY if details changed — marriage certificate (name), utility bill (address), or court order
  • A mobile number registered in your own name (for SMS tracking)

Step by step

  1. Choose online or in-person
    The Pak-ID app / id.nadra.gov.pk works if your name, father's name and date of birth are unchanged. Any change to your particulars needs an in-person NRC visit.
  2. Start the application
    Online: log in with your CNIC and upload a fresh photo. In-person: collect a token at any NADRA Registration Center (NRC) or Mega Center.
  3. Give biometrics (in-person only)
    Fingerprints and a live photo are re-captured at the counter. Skipped for pure online renewals where data is unchanged.
  4. Pick a processing speed & pay
    Choose Normal / Urgent / Executive and pay by card online or at the counter. Keep the receipt and your token number.
  5. Track your card
    SMS your 13-digit number to 8300, or track live in the Pak-ID app.
  6. Collect or receive it
    Delivered to your address by courier, or collect from the NRC on the due date if you chose in-person pickup.
